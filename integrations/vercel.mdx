# Vercel

Shanone's Vercel integration gives your agent 107 tools covering the full deployment lifecycle — projects, deployments, domains/DNS, environment variables, Edge Config, caching, and team/security administration.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Vercel (e.g. "list my Vercel projects"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Sign in with Vercel">
    Approve Vercel's OAuth consent screen for the team/account you want Shanone to act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `vercel_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="vercel_create_project or vercel_add_domain fails with a permissions error">
    Many project/domain/team tools operate on whichever team the connected Vercel account belongs to. Confirm the right team context with `vercel_list_teams` and `vercel_get_team` before assuming a permissions bug.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="vercel_redeploy or vercel_promote_deployment doesn't seem to take effect">
    Both operate on a specific deployment ID, not "the latest" deployment implicitly — call `vercel_list_deployments` first to confirm you're targeting the deployment you expect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Edge Config or cache changes aren't visible right away">
    Edge Config reads are served from a fast, eventually-consistent edge cache; `vercel_update_edge_config_items` can take a short time to propagate globally before `vercel_get_edge_config_items` reflects it everywhere.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OAuth connection expires unexpectedly">
    Vercel's OAuth tokens are short-lived; if a call fails with an auth error, reconnect from **Integrations** rather than assuming the token is permanent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
