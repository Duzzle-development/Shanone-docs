# Hacker News

Shanone's Hacker News integration gives your agent 7 tools for browsing every story feed Hacker News offers and drilling into a specific story's comments — all against the free, public Hacker News API, so there's nothing to authenticate.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="No connection needed">
    Hacker News's API is free and keyless — there's no account to connect or API key to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Just ask">
    Ask your agent to do something with Hacker News (e.g. "what's trending on HN right now?") and it will call `hackernews_*` tools directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally verify from Integrations">
    You can confirm the integration is listed as available under **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard, though no setup step is required there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Tools

Shanone provides **7 tools** for Hacker News, organized into these categories:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Story feeds">
    `hackernews_top_stories`, `hackernews_new_stories`, `hackernews_best_stories` — the front page, newest submissions, and all-time highly-rated stories
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Community feeds">
    `hackernews_ask_stories`, `hackernews_show_stories` — Ask HN discussion threads and Show HN project/product launches
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Jobs">
    `hackernews_job_stories` — YC and tech company job postings
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Story detail">
    `hackernews_get_story` — a specific story by ID, optionally with its top-level comments
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Story counts are lower than the count parameter I passed">
    `count` is capped at 30 per call, and any item that fails to load (deleted, or a transient API error) is silently skipped rather than retried — expect slightly fewer results than requested on a flaky connection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="hackernews_get_story returns comments but some are missing text">
    Deleted or flagged comments are filtered out entirely, and comment text is truncated at 500 characters — request the story again with a smaller `max_comments` if you want to reduce noise.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A story's url field is empty">
    Ask HN and some other self-post story types don't have an external URL — use the `hn_url` (or `text` field for Ask HN posts) instead of assuming every story links out.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
