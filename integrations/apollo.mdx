# Apollo

Shanone's Apollo integration gives your agent 40 tools for B2B sales workflows — contact and account CRM, people/organization search and enrichment, deals, tasks, and outreach sequences.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an Apollo API key">
    In Apollo, go to **Settings > Integrations > API Keys** and generate a key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Shanone">
    Ask your agent to do something with Apollo (e.g. "search for VP of Engineering contacts at Series B startups"), or add the API key proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once the key is stored, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `apollo_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="apollo_enrich_person or apollo_enrich_organization returns partial data">
    Apollo's enrichment coverage varies by how much public/first-party data exists for that person or company — not every field will be populated for every record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bulk tools (apollo_bulk_create_contacts, etc.) fail partway through">
    Check `apollo_get_api_usage` for rate limit or credit exhaustion — Apollo enforces per-plan limits on search, enrichment, and bulk write operations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="apollo_add_contacts_to_sequence succeeds but contacts never receive outreach">
    Confirm the target sequence is active with `apollo_activate_sequence` — contacts added to a paused or draft sequence won't be emailed until it's activated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
