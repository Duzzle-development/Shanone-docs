# DocuSign

Shanone's DocuSign integration gives your agent 144 tools covering the full eSignature lifecycle — envelopes, templates, recipients, documents, and the account/billing administration that most integrations leave out.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with DocuSign (e.g. "send this contract for signature"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the DocuSign account">
    Approve DocuSign's OAuth consent screen for the account you want Shanone to act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `docusign_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="docusign_send_envelope fails with a recipient routing error">
    Check `docusign_list_recipients` and `docusign_list_recipient_tabs` first — DocuSign requires signature/initial tabs to be anchored to a valid document and recipient before the envelope can be sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Template-based envelope is missing fields">
    Templates carry their own recipient/tab definitions; use `docusign_create_envelope_from_template` and only override recipient roles that need to change rather than rebuilding tabs from scratch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Billing or account-admin tools return a permissions error">
    Account settings, brands, and billing tools (`docusign_update_account_settings`, `docusign_get_billing_plan`, etc.) require an admin-level DocuSign user; verify the connected account has admin membership in `docusign_get_account_info`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
