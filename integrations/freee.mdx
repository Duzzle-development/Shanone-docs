# freee

Shanone's freee integration gives your agent 125 tools across freee's accounting and HR platform — deals, invoices/quotations, payroll, expense/approval workflows, and bank account reconciliation.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with freee (e.g. "list unpaid invoices from last month"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the freee account">
    Approve freee's OAuth consent screen for the company you want Shanone to act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `freee_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="freee_create_deal fails with a missing account_item error">
    Deals require valid `account_item_id` and `partner_id` references — look them up first with `freee_list_account_items` and `freee_list_partners` rather than guessing IDs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="freee_request_journal_download returns a status instead of the file">
    Journal exports are asynchronous — poll `freee_get_journal_status` until it reports completion, then call `freee_download_journal` to fetch the actual file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="HR tools (freee_hr_*) return a different company than accounting tools">
    freee treats accounting (`company_id`) and HR (`company_id` under the separate HR API) as distinct scopes even for the same business — verify you're passing the ID returned by `freee_hr_get_me`, not the accounting `freee_get_company` ID.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
