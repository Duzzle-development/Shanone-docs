# Webex

Shanone's Webex integration gives your agent 27 tools for scheduling meetings and managing the spaces (rooms), teams, and messages people collaborate in.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Webex (e.g. "schedule a meeting for tomorrow at 3pm"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Webex account">
    Approve Webex's OAuth consent screen for the account you want Shanone to act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `webex_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Tools

Shanone provides **27 tools** for Webex, organized into these categories:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Meetings">
    `webex_list_meetings`, `webex_get_meeting`, `webex_create_meeting`, `webex_update_meeting`, `webex_delete_meeting`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rooms">
    `webex_list_rooms`, `webex_get_room`, `webex_create_room`, `webex_update_room`, `webex_delete_room`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Memberships">
    `webex_list_memberships`, `webex_get_membership`, `webex_create_membership`, `webex_update_membership`, `webex_delete_membership`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Teams">
    `webex_list_teams`, `webex_get_team`, `webex_create_team`, `webex_update_team`, `webex_delete_team`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages">
    `webex_list_messages`, `webex_get_message`, `webex_send_message`, `webex_delete_message`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Users">
    `webex_get_me`, `webex_get_person`, `webex_list_people`
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="webex_create_membership or webex_send_message fails with a not found error">
    Room, team, and person identifiers in Webex are opaque IDs, not emails or names — use `webex_list_rooms`/`webex_list_people` (or `webex_get_person`) to resolve the correct ID first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="webex_delete_room or webex_delete_team appears to succeed but content still shows elsewhere">
    Deleting a room or team is a moderator-level action; if the connected account isn't a moderator of that space, the call may fail silently depending on Webex's permission response — verify with `webex_get_membership` first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Meeting attendee list looks incomplete">
    `webex_get_meeting` returns the scheduled meeting's core fields; invitee lists are managed separately and may require checking the meeting's associated room membership via `webex_list_memberships`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
