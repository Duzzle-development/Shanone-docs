# Google Meet

Shanone's Google Meet integration gives your agent 14 tools for creating meeting spaces and pulling structured data out of past conferences — participants, recordings, transcripts, and AI-generated smart notes.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Meet (e.g. "summarize yesterday's standup transcript"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Google account">
    Approve Google's OAuth consent screen for the account whose Meet data Shanone should access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `google_meet_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Tools

Shanone provides **14 tools** for Google Meet, organized into these categories:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Spaces">
    `google_meet_create_space`, `google_meet_get_space`, `google_meet_end_active_conference`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Conference records">
    `google_meet_list_conference_records`, `google_meet_get_conference_record`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Participants">
    `google_meet_list_participants`, `google_meet_get_participant`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recordings">
    `google_meet_list_recordings`, `google_meet_get_recording`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transcripts">
    `google_meet_list_transcripts`, `google_meet_get_transcript`, `google_meet_list_transcript_entries`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Smart notes">
    `google_meet_list_smart_notes`, `google_meet_get_smart_note` — Gemini-generated meeting summaries, when enabled on the Workspace account
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="google_meet_get_transcript or google_meet_get_smart_note returns nothing">
    Transcripts and smart notes are only generated when recording/transcription was enabled during the meeting and are subject to the Workspace admin's Meet feature settings — they aren't available retroactively for meetings that didn't have them turned on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="google_meet_list_conference_records returns an empty list for a space I know was used">
    Conference records are created per-instance of a meeting in a space; use `google_meet_get_space` first to confirm the space name/ID, then list records scoped to that space rather than assuming a global listing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="google_meet_create_space succeeds but the join link doesn't work for external users">
    Space access settings are controlled by the Workspace admin's Meet safety settings (e.g. "Only invited users can join without knocking") — this is configured outside Shanone in the Google Admin console.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
