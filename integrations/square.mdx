# Square

Shanone's Square integration gives your agent 140 tools covering orders, payments, invoicing, the product catalog, loyalty, subscriptions, and appointment bookings — enough to run most of a retail or service business's day-to-day commerce operations.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Square (e.g. "list today's payments over $50"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize your Square account">
    Approve Square's OAuth consent screen for the merchant account you want Shanone to act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `square_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="square_create_payment or square_create_order fails with an idempotency error">
    Square requires a unique `idempotency_key` per write call to prevent duplicate charges — check `shanone_get_tool_schema` for `square_create_payment` and generate a fresh key per attempt, not per retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Catalog writes via square_upsert_catalog_object don't show the expected result">
    Square's catalog uses a graph of typed objects (ITEM, ITEM_VARIATION, CATEGORY, etc.) with client-assigned temporary IDs for new objects — mismatched or reused temporary IDs are the most common cause of unexpected catalog state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="square_search_availability returns no open slots">
    Availability search depends on the business booking profile and team member schedules being configured correctly in Square first — use `square_retrieve_business_booking_profile` to confirm bookings are enabled before assuming the tool is broken.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A dispute action like square_accept_dispute fails">
    Disputes have a limited window and specific required states (e.g. evidence must be submitted before certain actions) — check `square_get_dispute` for the current state before acting on it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
