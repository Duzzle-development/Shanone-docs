# TikTok Ads

Shanone's TikTok Ads integration gives your agent 119 tools covering the full campaign hierarchy (campaigns → ad groups → ads), audience management, targeting dictionaries, creative assets, Business Center administration, and reporting — enough to run TikTok ad operations end-to-end from an agent.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with TikTok Ads (e.g. "show me the performance of my top 5 campaigns this week"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize your TikTok Ads account">
    Approve TikTok's OAuth consent screen for the Business Center or Ad Account you want Shanone to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `tiktok_ads_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="tiktok_ads_get_ad_review_info shows a rejected ad">
    Check the rejection reason in the response, fix the creative or targeting issue, then use `tiktok_ads_appeal_ad_rejection` if you believe the rejection was in error rather than recreating the ad from scratch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A reporting tool times out or returns partial data for a large date range">
    Use `tiktok_ads_create_async_report_task` instead of `tiktok_ads_run_sync_report` for large date ranges or high-cardinality breakdowns, then poll `tiktok_ads_get_async_report_task_status` and fetch results with `tiktok_ads_download_async_report`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="tiktok_ads_create_audience_by_file fails after uploading">
    Audience files must be uploaded with `tiktok_ads_upload_audience_file` first (PII pre-hashed with SHA-256), and the audience creation call must reference the returned file ID(s) — check `shanone_get_tool_schema` for the exact linkage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Targeting or budget update tools fail with a permissions error">
    Some operations (e.g. `tiktok_ads_create_bc_ad_account`, `tiktok_ads_process_payment`) require Business Center admin-level access, not just standard Ad Account access — check the connected account's role with `tiktok_ads_get_bc_members`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
