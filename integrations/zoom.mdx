# Zoom

Shanone's Zoom integration gives your agent 67 tools for meetings and webinars — scheduling, registrants, recordings/transcripts, polls/surveys, and the reporting tools most integrations skip.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Zoom (e.g. "schedule a meeting for 2pm tomorrow"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Zoom account">
    Approve Zoom's OAuth consent screen for the account you want Shanone to act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `zoom_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="zoom_get_meeting_summary or zoom_get_meeting_transcript returns nothing">
    AI Companion summaries and transcripts are only generated for meetings with cloud recording and the AI summary feature enabled at the account/meeting level — check `zoom_get_recording_settings` if a recent meeting has no summary.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="zoom_create_meeting succeeds but registrants can't join">
    If the meeting requires registration, registrants must be added via `zoom_add_meeting_registrant` and use their unique join link — the generic meeting URL alone won't work for a registration-required meeting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="zoom_get_past_meeting fails right after a meeting ends">
    Past-meeting data (participants, polls, Q&A) can take a few minutes to become available after a meeting ends — retry after a short delay rather than assuming the meeting ID is wrong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="zoom_delete_meeting_recordings doesn't free up storage immediately">
    Deleted cloud recordings may be retained in Zoom's trash for a recovery window before being permanently purged, depending on account settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
