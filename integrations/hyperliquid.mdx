# Hyperliquid

Shanone's Hyperliquid integration gives your agent 34 tools for the Hyperliquid perpetual DEX — account/portfolio state, market data (orderbooks, candles, funding), order management, leverage, and TWAP orders.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your wallet address">
    Copy the Ethereum wallet address you use (or plan to use) on Hyperliquid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to Shanone">
    Open **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard, select Hyperliquid, and enter your wallet address. This is enough for all read-only account and market data tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure a private key for trading (optional)">
    Placing or modifying orders requires a private key, which is configured separately as an environment variable rather than through the standard integration form — check with your Shanone admin if trading tools return an authentication error.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="hyperliquid_place_order or other trading tools fail with an authentication error">
    Read-only tools work with just a wallet address, but placing/modifying/canceling orders requires a private key configured server-side — confirm with your admin that trading is enabled for your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="hyperliquid_get_account_state shows different numbers than the Hyperliquid UI">
    Confirm you're checking the right subaccount with `hyperliquid_get_subaccounts` — perp and spot balances are tracked separately (`hyperliquid_get_account_state` vs `hyperliquid_get_spot_balances`), and it's easy to check the wrong one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="hyperliquid_place_twap_order behaves unexpectedly">
    TWAP orders execute over a specified duration in slices rather than immediately — use `hyperliquid_get_order_status` to track progress instead of expecting an instant fill, and `hyperliquid_cancel_twap_order` to stop it early if needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
