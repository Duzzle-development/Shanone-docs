# Typeform

Shanone's Typeform integration gives your agent 31 tools for building and updating forms, reading and clearing responses, managing themes/images, and organizing workspaces — a focused set for treating form creation and response handling as agent-driven tasks.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Typeform (e.g. "show me the latest responses to my feedback form"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize your Typeform account">
    Approve Typeform's OAuth consent screen for the account whose forms you want Shanone to access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `typeform_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Tools

Shanone provides **31 tools** for Typeform, organized into these categories:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Forms">
    `typeform_list_forms`, `typeform_get_form`, `typeform_create_form`, `typeform_update_form`, `typeform_patch_form`, `typeform_delete_form`, `typeform_get_form_messages`, `typeform_update_form_messages`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Responses">
    `typeform_list_responses`, `typeform_delete_responses`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Themes">
    `typeform_list_themes`, `typeform_get_theme`, `typeform_create_theme`, `typeform_update_theme`, `typeform_delete_theme`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Images">
    `typeform_list_images`, `typeform_get_image`, `typeform_upload_image`, `typeform_delete_image`, `typeform_get_image_by_size`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Webhooks">
    `typeform_list_webhooks`, `typeform_get_webhook`, `typeform_create_or_update_webhook`, `typeform_delete_webhook`, `typeform_list_webhook_deliveries`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Workspaces">
    `typeform_list_workspaces`, `typeform_get_workspace`, `typeform_create_workspace`, `typeform_update_workspace`, `typeform_delete_workspace`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Account">
    `typeform_get_me`
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="typeform_list_responses returns fewer results than expected">
    Responses are paginated and can be filtered by date/completion status — check `shanone_get_tool_schema` for `typeform_list_responses` and pass `since`/`until` or `completed` explicitly rather than assuming a single call returns everything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="typeform_update_form overwrites fields I didn't intend to change">
    `typeform_update_form` replaces the full form definition (PUT semantics) — use `typeform_patch_form` instead for partial updates to specific fields.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="typeform_create_or_update_webhook doesn't fire on new responses">
    Confirm the webhook tag is unique per form and that the form is actually receiving submissions — use `typeform_list_webhook_deliveries` to check recent delivery attempts and failures.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
