# Kling AI

Shanone's Kling AI integration gives your agent 6 tools for AI video generation — creating video from text or an image, extending existing clips, syncing lip movement to audio, and tracking generation tasks through to completion.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Kling AI Access Key and Secret Key">
    Generate an API key pair from your [Kling AI](https://klingai.com/) account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them to Shanone">
    Open **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard, select Kling AI, and enter your Access Key and Secret Key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once saved, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `kling_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Tools

Shanone provides **6 tools** for Kling AI, organized into these categories:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Video generation">
    `kling_text_to_video`, `kling_image_to_video` — generate a video clip from a text prompt or a starting image
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Video effects">
    `kling_extend_video`, `kling_lip_sync` — extend a previously generated clip, or sync a character's lip movement to an audio track
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Task tracking">
    `kling_get_task`, `kling_wait_for_task` — check a generation task's status once, or block until it completes (or times out) and return the resulting video URL
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="kling_wait_for_task times out on a longer generation">
    Video generation can take longer than the default wait window — increase `max_wait_seconds`, or fall back to polling `kling_get_task` yourself on a longer interval rather than blocking in one call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A task's status shows FAILED with no clear reason">
    Check the `error` field returned by `kling_get_task` — common causes are prompts that violate content policy or a source image that doesn't meet Kling's resolution/format requirements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="kling_lip_sync produces misaligned lip movement">
    Lip-sync quality depends on clean, isolated speech audio in the input track — background noise or overlapping speakers will degrade sync accuracy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
