# PayPal

Shanone's PayPal integration gives your agent 105 tools covering the full commerce lifecycle — orders and payments, invoicing, subscriptions/billing plans, dispute resolution, payouts, and webhook management.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with PayPal (e.g. "list my unpaid invoices from this month"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the PayPal account">
    Approve PayPal's OAuth consent screen for the business account Shanone should act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `paypal_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="paypal_capture_order fails with an order not approved error">
    The buyer must complete PayPal's approval flow before `paypal_capture_order` can succeed — check `paypal_get_order` for the order's current status first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="paypal_create_webhook events never seem to arrive">
    Confirm the webhook URL is publicly reachable and that `paypal_list_available_events` includes the event types you subscribed to; use `paypal_simulate_webhook_event` to test the endpoint directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="paypal_provide_dispute_evidence rejects the submission">
    Check `shanone_get_tool_schema` for the exact evidence payload shape — PayPal requires specific evidence types (e.g. `PROOF_OF_FULFILLMENT`) rather than free-form text or arbitrary attachments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
