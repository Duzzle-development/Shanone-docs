# Zendesk

Shanone's Zendesk integration gives your agent 64 tools for running customer support — tickets, users, organizations, groups, macros, and the satisfaction/SLA data most integrations skip.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Zendesk (e.g. "show me open tickets tagged urgent"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Zendesk subdomain and authorize">
    Provide your Zendesk subdomain (the `xxx` in `xxx.zendesk.com`) and approve Zendesk's OAuth consent screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `zendesk_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connecting asks for a subdomain I don't recognize">
    Your Zendesk subdomain is the part before `.zendesk.com` in your support URL (e.g. `acme` for `acme.zendesk.com`) — check your admin's browser URL if unsure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="zendesk_apply_macro_to_ticket doesn't change the ticket as expected">
    Macros can include conditional actions; call `zendesk_get_macro` first to review exactly what the macro will do before applying it, since its effect isn't always obvious from the name alone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="zendesk_search_tickets or zendesk_search_users returns unexpected results">
    Zendesk's search API uses its own query syntax (e.g. `status:open type:ticket`); a plain keyword string may not filter the way you expect — check Zendesk's search reference for supported operators.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="zendesk_merge_tickets fails">
    Merging requires both tickets to be open (not closed) and typically to belong to the same requester — verify both conditions with `zendesk_get_ticket` before merging.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
