# Google Sheets

Shanone's Google Sheets integration gives your agent 88 tools covering nearly the entire Sheets API — not just reading and writing values, but formatting, charts, filters, protected ranges, tables, and dimension management that most integrations leave out.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect via OAuth">
    Ask your agent to do something with Sheets (e.g. "add a chart to my Q3 budget sheet"), or connect proactively from **Integrations** in the Shanone dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Google account">
    Approve Google's OAuth consent screen for the account whose Sheets Shanone should access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry your request">
    Once connected, `shanone_execute_tool` calls for `google_sheets_*` tools will succeed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="google_sheets_update_values silently overwrites the wrong cells">
    Double-check the A1 notation range (e.g. `Sheet1!B2:D10`) — a missing sheet name defaults to the first sheet, which is a common source of accidental overwrites.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="google_sheets_add_chart or google_sheets_add_table fails with an invalid range error">
    Most structural tools (charts, tables, banding, protected ranges) require `sheetId` (a numeric ID) rather than the sheet name — call `google_sheets_get_spreadsheet` or `google_sheets_get_sheet_names` first to resolve it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Batch operations only partially apply">
    Batch tools like `google_sheets_batch_update_values` process each range independently; check the response for per-range errors rather than assuming an overall success means every range succeeded.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
