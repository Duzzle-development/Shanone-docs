## What is Shanone?

Shanone is the Command Center for Agent Harnesses, a central place to manage access to the tools and Agent Skills your AI agents use.

Connect to services such as Slack, GitHub, Notion, and Google Workspace from Cursor, Claude Code, or another [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/) client. Reuse a common set of tools and Skills across agents and execution environments, while managing access according to your team's policies.

## What you can do with Shanone

### Connect agents to external services

Use MCP to find services, retrieve information, and make updates across the tools your work depends on. Tasks that span multiple services can start with a conversation with your agent.

### Save and reuse Agent Skills

Save repeatable procedures and team knowledge as Skills. Agents read the Skills they need, follow their instructions, and use tools to carry out the work.

### Manage access to tools

Choose which services and tools each team member can use. Adjust permitted actions, such as reading or updating information, to fit each person's responsibilities.

### Customize tool parameters

Rename fields, remove unnecessary parameters, and set default values. Adapt the tool specifications exposed to your agents to suit your use case.

## Example workflows

- Review Datadog metrics and GitHub changes, then share incident investigation findings in Slack.
- Reconcile Stripe revenue data with an Airtable ledger and notify Slack of discrepancies.
- Extract action items from Notion meeting notes and create tasks in Linear.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Create a key on the [API Keys page](https://app.shanone.ai/api-keys) and configure Shanone in your MCP client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize services">
    Connect the external service accounts you want to use and grant the required access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your agent">
    Describe what you want to do in natural language. Your agent searches for the tools it needs, checks their inputs, and executes them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
